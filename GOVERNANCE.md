# Governance

judg3d is currently maintained by [Victor Sodre](https://github.com/victorsodre),
the primary maintainer. It is an early-stage project, not a foundation or a
multi-company initiative. AI tools assist development; they are not independent
human maintainers or evidence of community adoption.

The maintainer reviews contributions, decides release scope, handles security
reports and owns publishing permissions. Material product decisions should be
recorded in an issue, pull request or specification change with their rationale.
New acceptance checks must define profile-controlled thresholds and tests before
being advertised as available.

External contributors retain credit for their work. Additional maintainers can
be invited after sustained reviewed contributions and an explicit discussion of
responsibilities and access. There is no automatic access based on stars,
financial contributions or automated activity.

Only the current documented functionality is supported. Roadmap items are
proposals, not delivery promises. The project does not claim sponsorship,
partnership or endorsement from OpenAI, Khronos or other vendors.
